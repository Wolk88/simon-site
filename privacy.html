Privacy Policy

SiMon - System Monitor. Last updated: 9 August 2026.

SiMon does not collect any data about you. Nothing is sent anywhere, ever. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no advertising.

What the app reads

SiMon reads hardware readings from your Mac to display them in the menu bar: processor and memory load, graphics and Neural Engine activity, component temperatures and fan speed. These readings are used only to draw the interface. They are kept in memory for the recent history graph and are discarded when the app quits. They are never written to a file and never leave your Mac.

What the app stores

Your preferences, such as which metrics are visible, the polling interval and graph colours, are stored locally on your Mac by the standard macOS preferences system. They contain no personal information and are removed when you delete the app.

Network

SiMon makes no network connections. It does not contact any server, including ours. The app works identically with networking disabled.

Third parties

SiMon contains no third-party frameworks, SDKs or trackers. No data is shared with anyone, because none is gathered in the first place.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
